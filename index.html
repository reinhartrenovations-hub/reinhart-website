Who you're hiring

The guy who writes your estimate is on your job.

I'm Drew Reinhart. I run Reinhart Renovations out of northeast Iowa, and I do the takeoffs, order the materials, and work alongside the crew. That's how I know the number on your estimate is one we can actually build to.

I've been around construction projects since I was 10. Right out of high school I went through the carpentry program at Northeast Iowa Community College (NICC), and I took extra classes in building codes and estimating. I've also completed a 120-hour home inspection course.

Before starting Reinhart Renovations, I worked for five other building contractors so I could learn the best building practices from each of them. With those crews I built new homes, hog barns and additions, replaced windows and doors, and much more. I'd rather tell you what a job really takes up front than win it with a low number and make it up later.
